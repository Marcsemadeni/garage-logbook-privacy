Garage Logbook Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy applies to the Garage Logbook mobile app for Android (package com.garagelogbook.app), referred to below as "the app".

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app lets you keep records about your vehicles, such as cars, maintenance history, parts, and reminders. This information is stored only in a local database on your device. The app has no user accounts and does not ask you to sign in.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your data to any server operated by the developer.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

Device backups

Android's Auto Backup feature may copy the app's data to your own Google account as part of your device backup, so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or change devices. This is controlled by your device's backup settings, not by the developer, and the developer cannot access these backups. You can turn this off in your device's settings under Google → Backup.

Deleting your data

You can remove all data the app has stored by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding accounts or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to garagelogbook.app@gmail.com.
